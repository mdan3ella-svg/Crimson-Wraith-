A silhouette engineered
for the imagination.

An elongated spearhead nose, sculpted crimson surfaces and outboard propulsion housings establish the XV-01’s forward stance. Exposed underbody machinery contrasts with the flowing upper shell; bright light ribbons trace its aerodynamic edges.

The supplied FBX is the source of this interactive model. Explore its original geometry, inspect the underside and separate its authored assemblies. Distances and mechanical motion are illustrative; the source provides no certified scale, performance data or joint constraints.

05 / ORIGINAL VIEWS
Model analysis & animation scope

15 mesh objects; 474,672 triangles; no authored animation clips or skeleton. The primary body combines the canopy, fins and multiple surfaces. These remain intact. Assembly separation moves the original objects; the small central support assembly receives a conceptual vertical retraction. Twin exhaust effects are presentation additions.
